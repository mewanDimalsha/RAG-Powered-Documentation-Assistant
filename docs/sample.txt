Refund Policy
Our refund policy allows customers to return items within 30 days.
Items must be unopened and in original condition.
To request a refund, contact support@company.com.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded.
Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item was defective.

Privacy Policy
We do not sell customer data to third parties.
All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256.
Users can request data deletion at any time by emailing privacy@company.com.
We retain logs for 90 days for security purposes.
Cookies are used only for session management and analytics.

Payment Policy
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal.
All payments are processed through Stripe.
Invoices are issued automatically on purchase.
For enterprise billing, contact billing@company.com.
Payment terms for enterprise customers are net-30.

Support Policy
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm GMT.
Response time for standard tickets is 24 hours.
Priority support is available for enterprise customers.
Critical issues are escalated within 2 hours.
You can reach support at support@company.com or via live chat.

Data Retention Policy
Customer data is retained for 7 years for compliance purposes.
Deleted accounts have their data anonymised within 30 days.
Audit logs are immutable and retained for 5 years.
Backups are performed daily and stored for 90 days.
We do not retain data for users who have requested deletion.
